Shri Krishna Plastic

We make footwear and soles in EVA, PU, AIR and Injection. Choose a process to see what it is best for.

EVA

Ethylene-vinyl acetate

A light, soft and cushioned foam. It is the everyday choice for comfortable footwear that is easy to wear for long hours.

  • Slippers
  • Sandals
  • Flip-flops
  • Midsoles

PU

Polyurethane

A tough, flexible material that resists wear. It gives soles a firm grip and a long working life.

  • Shoe soles
  • Casual shoes
  • Formal shoes
  • Work footwear

AIR

Air-cushioned footwear

Air-blown construction keeps the sole light while adding spring and comfort with every step.

  • Light soles
  • Comfort footwear
  • Daily wear

Injection

Injection moulding

Material is injected into a mould, so every pair comes out with the same shape and finish. It suits large, repeat orders.

  • Bulk orders
  • Uniform finish
  • Moulded footwear
